HPIC Financial Snapshot · Last updated 14 August 2026

What this tool is

HPIC Financial Snapshot is an internal dashboard built for the board of the Highland Park Improvement Club (HPIC), a volunteer-run nonprofit in Seattle, Washington. It displays HPIC's own financial position — cash balances and grant status — drawn from the systems HPIC already uses as its records of account.

Who may use it

Use is limited to HPIC board members and others the board authorizes. Access requires a shared passphrase issued by HPIC. The tool is not offered to the public, not licensed or distributed to anyone else, and not sold. HPIC may change or withdraw access at any time.

Read-only by design

The tool only reads data. It does not create, modify, or delete records in QuickBooks Online, Little Green Light, or any bank, and it cannot move money or initiate any transaction.

No warranty; not financial advice

The tool is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. Figures shown are for orientation only and may be incomplete, delayed, or affected by an upstream service being unavailable. The dashboard is not a substitute for HPIC's books, and it does not constitute accounting, tax, investment, or financial advice.

The underlying systems of record govern. Any decision with financial consequence should be verified against QuickBooks Online, Little Green Light, and the relevant grant agreements — not against this dashboard alone. Grant figures reflect only what has been entered into Little Green Light and may not represent a complete grant history.

Acceptable use

Do not share the passphrase outside the group HPIC has authorized, and do not attempt to use the tool to access data belonging to any organization other than HPIC.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the tool develops. The date above reflects the most recent revision.
