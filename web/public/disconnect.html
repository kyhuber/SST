Disconnect QuickBooks

HPIC Financial Snapshot reads HPIC's QuickBooks Online balances. Disconnecting stops that access immediately and permanently until it is re-authorized.

How to disconnect

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator.
  2. Go to Apps → My Apps.
  3. Find SST_App and choose Disconnect.

What happens next

The stored authorization becomes invalid at once. The dashboard will stop showing balances and will state plainly that QuickBooks is no longer connected — it will not display stale figures as though they were current.

Reconnecting

An HPIC administrator can restore access by visiting the dashboard's authorization endpoint with the shared passphrase. Reconnecting requires signing in to QuickBooks and granting consent again.
